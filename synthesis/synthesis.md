# Task 4: Synthesis and Integration

Integrating automated scripting, strategic firewall design, and sustainable IT lifecycle management establishes an efficient, secure, and eco-friendly computer laboratory ecosystem. Automation through PowerShell scripts significantly reduces manual administrative workloads by instantly gathering core diagnostics, OS health, and disk utilization across all 30 workstations. This proactive monitoring enables administrators to detect storage bottlenecks and maintenance needs early, preventing unplanned downtime and extending equipment longevity.

Simultaneously, a robust firewall configuration protects these hardware investment assets. Restricting external traffic exclusively to public HTTPS web services while isolating administrative remote access isolates internal workstations from network attacks, malware infections, and unauthorized tampering. Preventing cybersecurity breaches protects device operational integrity and eliminates premature software re-installations or drive failures.

Finally, aligned with sustainable IT policies, operational insights gathered from administrative scripts inform targeted upgrades—such as replacing mechanical drives with solid-state drives—rather than prematurely discarding functional desktops. Re-imaging older machines with lightweight operating systems further reduces electronic waste and hardware procurement costs. Together, scripting streamlines daily management, firewall security protects infrastructure investments, and lifecycle policies ensure sustainable, long-term environmental and financial responsibility.
